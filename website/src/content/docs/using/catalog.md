---
title: The catalogue
description: Browse and search the folders that are shared with you.
---

The catalogue shows the folders of the operator's media shares that are granted to you: browse level by level, or
search by folder name. Shares granted to everyone and shares granted only to you look the same.

**Status:** the read-only catalogue arrives with 0.1.0. Requests from the catalogue follow with 0.3.0.
