---
title: Using Tesserarr
description: What a friend does with Tesserarr, from the invitation to the catalogue.
---

You got an invitation from someone who runs Tesserarr. With it you create your account, sign in and browse the folders
they share with you.

1. [Sign in](/tesserarr/using/sign-in/): redeem the invitation and choose how you sign in.
2. [Your account](/tesserarr/using/account/): passkeys, second factor, recovery codes and API tokens.
3. [The catalogue](/tesserarr/using/catalog/): what is shared with you.

Requests from the catalogue arrive with 0.3.0, the files themselves travel from 0.2.0 on.
