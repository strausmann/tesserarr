## Navigation

On a screen at least 1024 pixels wide the navigation is a fixed sidebar on the left. On a narrower screen, such as a
phone, a bar at the top shows the menu button, the logo and the title of the page. The menu button opens the whole
navigation as a drawer: your account, sign-out and the running version are at its foot. Escape, a tap beside the drawer
or a link inside it closes it again.

A friend sees the catalogue, their tokens and their account. The operator also sees status, media, operations and
administration.

## Language

The interface is in English and German. Without a choice of your own, it follows the language of your browser; a
language other than these two gives English. To fix the language for every device you sign in on, open
**Settings → Appearance → Language**. "As the browser says" hands the choice back to the browser.

## Collapsed sections

Some sections fold away with a click on their heading. Tesserarr remembers what you folded, on the server, so the
section stays folded on your other devices too.

## Empty views

A view without data says in one sentence what is missing and, where you can change that, offers the action that does.

## On a phone

Fields keep the keyboard that fits them: an email field the email keyboard, a code field the number pad. The return key
moves to the next field and sends the form from the last one. With the keyboard open, the field, its list and the main
buttons of a dialog stay in sight. A cross in a search or name field clears it.
