---
title: Plex
description: Connect a Plex Media Server to read its libraries.
---

A Plex connection reads the libraries of a Plex Media Server; you can add several servers. Plex is optional: Tesserarr
works on the folders of your media shares, with or without Plex. Signing in with a Plex account is a separate
[sign-in method](/tesserarr/admin/login-methods/).

**Status:** this connection arrives with 0.1.0.
