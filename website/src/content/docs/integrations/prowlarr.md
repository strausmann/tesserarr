---
title: Prowlarr
description: Connect Prowlarr and, from 0.3.0, offer Tesserarr as an indexer to your friends.
---

In 0.1.0 a Prowlarr connection is a connection test with the address and the API key of the instance. From 0.3.0 your
friends can add Tesserarr to their own Prowlarr as a Newznab indexer, which hands it on to their Sonarr and Radarr.

**Status:** this connection arrives with 0.1.0.
