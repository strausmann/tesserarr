Tesserarr is in development. The first release, 0.1.0, brings accounts, sign-in, shares, the catalogue and the
connections to Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, Syncthing and Plex. Files start to travel with 0.2.0. Every page says what it
covers today and what follows.

## What it does

- **You decide who sees what.** Friends get an account only through an invitation. You grant them folders of your media
  shares, as deep as you like.
- **Your friends ask the way they already do.** From 0.3.0 their Sonarr or Radarr finds your catalogue like any other
  indexer; without arr apps they request a title in the web interface.
- **Files travel over Syncthing.** The link between your homelab and theirs already exists, or Tesserarr sets it up.
  Hardlinks come first, so a title shared with several friends takes its space once.
- **One centre, optional satellites.** The central instance holds the users, the rules and the audit log. A satellite
  next to your media storage only does the file work, and has no database.
